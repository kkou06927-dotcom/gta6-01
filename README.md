**Solmar Bay Heat** — an original top-down open-world crime game set in the sun-soaked coastal city of Solmar Bay. You are Rico Marín, a broke wheelman who owes harbour smuggler Ilya Brandt $5,000. Fixer Dario Fenn has three jobs that will clear the debt: a dock pickup, a gold coupe to steal back from Palms Beach Car Park, and one last bank score with an escape to the Cliffside Hideout.

Explore the whole city on foot or behind the wheel — a road grid with AI traffic driving real lanes, pedestrians who stroll and flee, parked cars in every lot, a beach, a promenade and working docks. Any car can be stolen (the driver bails out and runs). Crimes raise your wanted stars from 0 to 5; the SBPD gives chase, rams you, and fills a bust meter when they pin you — get busted and you respawn at Police HQ minus a fine, get flattened and you wake up at Solmar General. Combat is mouse-aimed shooting and punches, health regenerates when things quiet down, and the HUD carries health, cash and debt, wanted stars, your current objective with distance and edge arrow, a speedometer, context hints, and a live minimap with objective marker and police blips. Progress saves to the browser automatically.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Controls: WASD or arrow keys to move and drive, E to steal or exit a car, mouse to aim, click (or hold) to shoot, Space to punch, Shift to sprint, P or Esc to pause. On the title screen, New Game starts fresh and Continue picks up your saved run. Tip: append `?demo=city`, `?demo=drive`, `?demo=chase` or `?demo=mission` to the URL to jump straight into a staged scene.
