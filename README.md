**Solmar Bay Heat** — an original top-down open-world crime game in a sun-soaked coastal city, drawn entirely with canvas (no assets, no network). You are Rico Marín, a broke wheelman working off a debt: explore Solmar Bay on foot or by car, steal rides, and run three story missions for fixer Dario Fenn — a dockside delivery, a gold coupe heist, and a bank getaway. AI traffic drives the road network, pedestrians stroll (and flee), and the Solmar Bay PD chases, rams and busts you as your wanted stars rise with every crime and fade when you lie low. HUD with health, cash, wanted stars, objective banner and a live minimap; pause, busted/downed respawns, and save/continue via localStorage. Demo scenes for a quick look: add `?demo=city`, `?demo=drive`, `?demo=chase` or `?demo=mission` to the URL.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Controls: WASD/arrows move and drive, Shift run, E enter/exit car, mouse aim + click to shoot, Space punch (handbrake in a car), P/Esc pause.
